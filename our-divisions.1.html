OUR DIVISIONS

SFIO was formed by like-minded entrepreneurs who have decided to form an “alliance”

SFIO was formed by like-minded entrepreneurs who have decided to form an “alliance” or group of companies with unique capabilities gained over 30 years. The collective capabilities and synergistic business models of companies within SFIO are aimed to support and enable the strategic expansion and growth of Epiphany brand globally. At the same time, internal growth of each company within the conglomerate will be supported by SFIO.
Meet the Fleet
What started as an epiphany, is now a global ecosystem of emerging, high-growth businesses
SFIO Founder and CEO Jeths Lacson built his career in engineering. But his heart has been in F&B since 2015, when he founded Epiphany Cafe, the flagship brand of our F&B division. Since its opening in Hamilton, New Zealand, Epiphany has scaled nationwide through strategic partnerships and growth partners. That same ecosystem approach forms the backbone of Starfleet Innotech's philosophy as an investment holding company. Today, SFIO manages a global portfolio of highly-collaborative businesses across F&B, Real Estate, and Technology.

Our Philosophy

Assembling Starfleet

Our goal at SFIO is to find promising businesses across emerging markets, forge transformative synergies across them, and help them scale through our global network. To do this, we’ve established a comprehensive strategy anchored in cross-divisional collaborations.

We strategically enter new markets through our F&B businesses, led by our flagship brand, Epiphany Cafe. We fund this growth through real estate development projects, spearheaded by Moraya, our premier real estate brand for tech-enabled communities championing wellness and sustainability. And we augment operations and unlock new opportunities through our technology division, LNS+. Through this ecosystem we’ve built, we have a number of support services ranging from accounting, to digital marketing, to architecture and engineering, to website development and project management.
